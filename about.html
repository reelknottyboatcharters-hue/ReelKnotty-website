About the Captain

Captain Rich Conolly — Reel Knotty Boat Charters, Long Island, New York.

Rich runs Reel Knotty out of Seaford, on Long Island's South Shore. The boat is set up for two kinds of days: easy beach charters where your group anchors up, swims, floats, and grills — and real fishing trips for up to six anglers.

Beach charters take up to 10 people, with hot dogs and hamburgers included. If you want to step it up, add the wagyu package — two wagyu steaks, prime rib or NY strip, grilled on the boat.

When he's not running charters, Rich builds custom fishing rods as Rods by Rich and runs GWTF Boat & Backyard Shrink Wrapping. The water is what he does.
